Academic Writing Protocol
When invoked to write or revise the research paper, you must follow these strict guidelines:

Phase 1: Data Ingestion
Read all experimental outputs, statistical summaries, and charts located in the results/ directory.

Do not infer or invent data. If a requested metric is missing, halt and instruct the @data_scientist to compute it.

Phase 2: Drafting
Structure the document using standard academic formatting: Abstract, Introduction, Methodology, Results, Discussion, and Conclusion.

All factual claims must be immediately followed by a placeholder for a citation or a direct reference to the generated data.

Maintain an objective, formal academic tone. Do not use conversational filler.
